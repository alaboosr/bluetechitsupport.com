About BlueTech IT Support

BlueTech IT Support Inc. is a Toronto IT company founded by Rafid Alaboosi, a senior IT specialist with over two decades of hands-on experience. Rafid has kept critical systems running at some of Canada's largest organizations, including Scotiabank and Shared Services Canada, and now brings that same experience to businesses of every size.

Our approach is simple: understand your business first, then recommend technology that fits. Every solution has to be technically sound and make sense for your goals and budget.

When something breaks, you need someone who stays calm under pressure. Rafid has provided 24/7 support for critical production servers, where downtime isn't an option. You get that same reliability.
